\section{Experimental and reproducibility details}

\subsection{Synthetic calculations}
The exact-risk script truncates at a large $M$ and adds the Hurwitz-zeta tail,
so no finite-dimensional tail is silently discarded. Slopes are fit only on
the largest scales and local slopes are retained. The independent block Monte
Carlo sampler draws directly from the infinite Zipf innovation law. The
raw-horizon sampler concatenates deterministic integer dwell blocks and stops
at exactly $N$ observations. The heavy-tail experiment uses
$(a,b,r)=(2,5.5,3)$, where the block duration has infinite variance and the
stationary residual exponent is $(a-1)/r=1/3$.

The noisy experiment evaluates
$h_B(q)=\E[\1\{K>0\}/K]$ by Gauss--Legendre quadrature using
\[
h_B(q)=\int_0^1\frac{(1-q+qt)^B-(1-q)^B}{t}\,dt,
\]
and compares the coverage-limited choice $M\asymp B^{1/(a+r)}$ with direct
minimization over $M$.

\subsection{Dense representation}
The dense test uses $D=512$ modes. A Haar-like orthogonal dictionary checks the
exact change-of-basis identity. The harder dictionary is a row-normalized
mixture of the orthogonal matrix and an i.i.d. Gaussian perturbation. Its
measured condition number is 4.67 and maximum normalized row inner product is
0.13. The learner is the ambient minimum-norm interpolant on seen mode
equations; it is not given the latent decoder. Results average 120 datasets per
scale.

The dense Gaussian AR negative control has the same marginal diagonal spectrum
but observes all coordinates at every time:
\[
x_{t,j}=\rho_jx_{t-1,j}
+\sqrt{1-\rho_j^2}\sqrt{\lambda_j}z_{t,j},
\qquad \rho_j=e^{-1/j^r}.
\]
We fit the noiseless batch minimum-norm predictor and evaluate population risk
under $\operatorname{diag}(\lambda)$. Forty trials are used for each
$N\in\{16,24,36,54,80,120,176\}$ at dimension 256. This control is intended
to reject an overgeneralized theorem, not to establish an alternative
asymptotic law.

\subsection{Appliance-energy diagnostic}
We use the chronological appliance-energy measurements of
\citet{candanedo2017appliances}, sampled every ten minutes. After removing two
random surrogate columns, we add calendar features, six physical-feature and
target lags (10 minutes through one day), standardize using the chronological
training split, and fit PCA followed by ridge regression. Ridge strength is
selected on the final 20\% of the training period. Learning curves average 12
randomly located contiguous windows and 12 random subsets at each size. The
last 20\% of time is always held out for testing.

For PCA scores $z_{t,j}$, integrated autocorrelation times use Geyer's initial
positive sequence, truncated at two days. Target energy is
$\widehat s_j\propto\widehat\lambda_j\widehat\theta_j^2$. Both spectral
proxies fit exactly two nuisance parameters--a global amplitude and rate--on
$N\le1024$ and are evaluated without refitting on $N\ge2048$. The random-subset
comparison is a falsification control: a genuinely temporal correction should
not necessarily improve data whose order has been destroyed.

\subsection{Reproduction}
All synthetic experiments require only NumPy, SciPy, Matplotlib, and
scikit-learn. The default synthetic suite uses $O(D^2)$ memory only for the
dense dictionary and runs without a GPU. Commands, exact seeds, unit tests,
CSV tables, JSON summaries, and figure-generation scripts are included in the
repository. The real-data workflow downloads the public source file, records
its URL, and uploads all outputs as a CI artifact.
