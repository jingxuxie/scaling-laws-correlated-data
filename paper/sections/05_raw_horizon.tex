\section{From Innovations to Raw Trajectories}

The exact identity counts completed innovations. We next observe exactly $N$
raw examples from integer-duration blocks. Let $\widehat\theta^{(N)}$ memorize
each noiseless mode encountered before time $N$.

\begin{theorem}[Raw horizon and inspection paradox]
\label{thm:raw}
Assume Equation~\eqref{eq:power-laws}, $\sigma=0$, and
$1<b<a+r+1$. If the stream starts at a renewal boundary, then for every
$r\ge0$,
\begin{equation}
\E\risk(\widehat\theta^{(N)},\theta)
\asympconst M^{-(b-1)}+N^{-(b-1)/(a+r)}.
\label{eq:raw-boundary}
\end{equation}
No finite-variance dwell assumption is needed. Under stationary equilibrium
initialization, for $r>0$,
\begin{align}
\E\risk(\widehat\theta^{(N)}_{\rm eq},\theta)
\asympconst{}&M^{-(b-1)}+N^{-(b-1)/(a+r)}\nonumber\\
&+N^{-(a-1)/r}.
\label{eq:raw-stationary}
\end{align}
For $r=0$, the last term is absent.
\end{theorem}

For mode $j$, let $H_j$ be the raw start time of its first block. A geometric
failure calculation gives
\begin{align}
\E H_j&=\mu/q_j-\tau_j\le\mu/q_j,\nonumber\\
(1-q_j)^N&\le\Prob(H_j\ge N)
\le\min\{1,\mu/(Nq_j)\}.
\label{eq:hitting-sandwich}
\end{align}
Summing against $s_j$ at $j\asymp N^{1/(a+r)}$ proves
Equation~\eqref{eq:raw-boundary}. In equilibrium, the initial block is
size-biased. Its residual life $R$ obeys
$\Prob(R\ge N)\asymp N^{-(a-1)/r}$, producing the additional term in
Equation~\eqref{eq:raw-stationary}. This is a genuine inspection-paradox phase,
not a finite-sample constant.

Noise can be transferred without requiring $\E\tau_J^2<\infty$. Let
$D=\tau_J$ for $J\sim q$ and $\beta=(b-1)/(a+r)$.

\begin{theorem}[Noisy raw horizons from fractional moments]
\label{thm:noisy-raw}
Let $1<b\le a+r$, $\sigma=O(1)$, and restrict estimates to the first $M$
modes. Set $B_N=\lfloor N/(2\mu)\rfloor$. If $r=0$, or if
\begin{equation}
\frac{b-1}{a+r}<\frac{a-1}{r},
\label{eq:fractional-condition}
\end{equation}
then, over the signed class $\Theta_s$ of Theorem~\ref{thm:minimax},
\begin{equation}
\inf_{\widetilde\theta}\sup_{\theta\in\Theta_s}
\E\risk(\widetilde\theta,\theta)
\asympconst M^{-(b-1)}+N^{-\beta}.
\label{eq:noisy-raw-rate}
\end{equation}
The upper bound is attained by using the first $B_N$ completed blocks when
they fit in the horizon and outputting zero otherwise.
\end{theorem}

For $r>0$, $\E D^\nu<\infty$ exactly for
$\nu<1+(a-1)/r$. Under Equation~\eqref{eq:fractional-condition}, choose
$1+\beta<\nu\le2$ below this threshold. The von Bahr--Esseen inequality gives
$\Prob(\sum_{k\le B_N}D_k>N)=O(N^{1-\nu})=o(N^{-\beta})$. On the completion
event, Theorem~\ref{thm:scaling} applies with $B_N\asymp N$; its variance term
is absorbed when $b\le a+r$. The unseen-sign prior from
Theorem~\ref{thm:minimax} gives the matching lower bound. This strictly extends
the finite-variance range $r<a-1$.

Finally, a reversible Markov realization on states $(j,s)$ refreshes at rate
$\tau_j^{-1}$ and otherwise stays put. Its stationary law is
$\lambda_j/2$, and
\begin{equation}
\E[X_tX_{t+\ell}^\top]
=\sum_j\lambda_j(1-\tau_j^{-1})^\ell e_je_j^\top.
\label{eq:nonseparable-cov}
\end{equation}
Different modes carry different temporal kernels, so the covariance is
nonseparable unless all $\tau_j$ coincide.
