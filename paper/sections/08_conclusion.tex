\section{Conclusion}

We separated the marginal spectrum of a task from the innovation spectrum of a
persistent stream. Their mismatch yields an exact, minimax scaling law in
which mode-dependent persistence changes the data exponent, while uniform
persistence changes only constants. Raw-horizon, heavy-tail, noise, dense
representation, and negative-control results identify both the reach and the
boundary of this mechanism. The broader lesson is that scaling with dependent
data is governed by how quickly new predictive directions arrive, not by one
scalar count of correlated observations.
