# AI-assisted research record

An AI assistant was used during model design, proof exploration, proof auditing,
code generation, numerical validation, literature organization, manuscript
editing, and build automation. The human authors remain responsible for every
claim, proof, experiment, citation, disclosure, and submission decision. The AI
system is not an author or a citable scientific source.

The automated research pass specifically:

- proposed and checked the spectral-renewal construction;
- drafted exact-risk, minimax, raw-horizon, fractional-moment, representation,
  and negative-control arguments;
- generated and tested experiment implementations;
- assembled a venue-formatted draft and reproducibility workflow.

Before submission, the authors must independently:

1. verify every theorem and appendix derivation line by line;
2. rerun the complete workflow from a clean environment;
3. inspect all retained outputs and final PDFs;
4. check every bibliographic entry against the primary source;
5. document AI use exactly as required by AAAI at submission time; and
6. remove or revise any statement that is not supported by a completed proof,
   experiment, or clearly labeled empirical observation.
