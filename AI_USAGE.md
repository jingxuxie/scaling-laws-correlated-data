# AI-assisted research record

This project used an AI assistant during initial model design, proof drafting,
code generation, literature organization, and manuscript editing. The human
authors remain responsible for every claim, proof, experiment, citation, and
submission decision.

Before submission, the authors should independently:

1. verify every theorem and appendix derivation line by line;
2. rerun all experiments from a clean environment;
3. check every bibliographic entry against the primary source;
4. document AI use in the manner required by the target venue at submission
   time; and
5. remove or revise any claim not supported by a completed proof or experiment.

The current draft intentionally labels important scope limitations, especially
the distinction between persistent coordinate sampling and arbitrary dense
Gaussian time series.
